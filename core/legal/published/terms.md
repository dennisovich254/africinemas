# AfriCinemas terms of use

These terms apply when you use AfriCinemas: a cinema's website on AfriCinemas (its
**storefront**), AfriCinemas through an AI app you connect, or
`africinemas.co.ke`. Please read them with the cinema's own terms of sale and refund
policy, which are linked from its storefront's footer.

## 1. Who we are, and who sells you your tickets

1.1 AfriCinemas is being registered as a company in Kenya; its registered name, number
and office will be added here. Contact: help@africinemas.co.ke.

1.2 **The cinema sells you your tickets, not AfriCinemas.** Each storefront belongs to
a cinema. When you book, your contract is with that cinema, for its show, seats, snacks
and 3D glasses. AfriCinemas provides the website and booking system for the cinema, and
takes your booking and starts your M-Pesa payment on the cinema's behalf. Your payment
goes to the cinema.

1.3 The cinema's name, address and contacts are on its storefront. The cinema's own
**terms of sale**, **refund and cancellation policy**, **house rules** and **privacy
policy** apply to your booking. If they conflict with these terms about your booking,
the cinema's terms apply, except that nothing takes away your rights under Kenyan law.

1.4 "Verified by AfriCinemas" on a storefront means AfriCinemas checked the cinema's
owner and business documents before it could sell. It doesn't mean AfriCinemas
guarantees the cinema's shows or service. You can check a cinema at
`africinemas.co.ke/verified`.

## 2. Who can use AfriCinemas

2.1 You must be **18 or over** to create an account or pay. A child can come to the
cinema, but a parent or guardian books and pays for them (Data Protection Act, 2019,
s.33).

2.2 You give true details when you book: an email address you use, and the M-Pesa
number you pay from.

## 3. Accounts

3.1 You can book without an account. An account lets you see your tickets in one place
and save time.

3.2 **An account belongs to one cinema.** If you book at two cinemas on AfriCinemas,
you have a separate account at each.

3.3 Keep your password private, and don't let anyone else use your account. Tell the
cinema, or us, at once if you think someone has. Changing your password doesn't yet
sign you out on other devices, so sign out on them yourself.

3.4 You can set your name and change your password on **My account**. You can ask the
cinema to delete your account (see its privacy policy, and ours).

## 4. Booking and paying

4.1 **What happens when you book** (Consumer Protection Act, s.31):

1. you choose a showtime, seats, and if you like snacks and 3D glasses;
2. **your seats are held for you for a few minutes** while you finish; if you don't
   pay in time, they're released;
3. **you see your order before you pay**: the showtime, the seats, every item and the
   full price, including any booking fee, with a link to the cinema's terms and refund
   policy. You can go back and change anything, or leave without paying;
4. you enter your M-Pesa number and press **Pay**. **Pressing Pay is accepting the
   order** on the cinema's terms;
5. M-Pesa asks you to confirm the payment on your phone with your PIN. **Never share
   your PIN**: neither AfriCinemas nor the cinema will ever ask for it;
6. once M-Pesa confirms the payment, your booking is confirmed and your tickets are
   shown and emailed to you.

4.2 **Your copy** (Consumer Protection Act, s.32). The confirmation email is your copy
of your booking: what you bought, the price, the cinema's details and a link to the
terms you agreed to, in the version you agreed to. Keep it, or print it.

4.3 **Prices** are in Kenya shillings and include everything you pay. The cinema sets
them.

4.4 **If the payment fails or you don't confirm it**, no money is taken, and your seats
are released when the hold ends. If money left your M-Pesa but your booking wasn't
confirmed, contact the cinema with your M-Pesa receipt: the cinema will confirm your
booking or refund you.

4.5 **At the box office**, the cinema's staff use AfriCinemas to sell you tickets and
snacks; the same terms apply, and you get your tickets on paper or by email.

## 5. Your tickets

5.1 Each ticket has a QR code that's scanned at the door once. Don't share it: whoever
shows it first is let in.

5.2 Snacks and glasses bought with tickets come with a pickup code, shown at the snack
counter.

5.3 You can add snacks to a booking until the showtime starts, through your tickets
link or at the box office.

## 6. Refunds, changes and cancelled shows

6.1 Refunds and exchanges follow **the cinema's refund and cancellation policy**,
which you can read before you pay.

6.2 **If the cinema cancels a show**, it refunds you or offers you another show, as its
policy and the law require.

6.3 **Your rights under Kenyan law stay.** Nothing in these terms or the cinema's
takes away your rights under the Consumer Protection Act (including to services of
reasonably merchantable quality, s.5) or the Sale of Goods Act (for snacks and goods).

## 7. Age ratings

7.1 Every film shows its Kenya Film Classification Board rating. The cinema must not
admit a child to a film rated for adults only, and it may check ages at the door
(Films and Stage Plays Act, s.17). If you're refused entry because a ticket holder is
under the film's age, the cinema's refund policy applies.

## 8. AI apps

8.1 You can connect an AI app (such as Claude) to book at a cinema for you. It acts
only with your account at that cinema, and only after you sign in and agree. You can
disconnect it at any time on **My account**. The app's own terms and privacy policy
also apply to your use of it.

8.2 **An AI app can make mistakes.** Check the showtime, seats and price on the order
before you pay. The order shown before Pay is what you're buying. An AI app never pays
for you: you always confirm a payment yourself, on your phone.

## 9. Using AfriCinemas fairly

You don't:

- use AfriCinemas for anything unlawful, including under the Computer Misuse and
  Cybercrimes Act, 2018;
- try to get into other people's accounts or bookings, or test or attack AfriCinemas'
  security (if you find a weakness, please tell us at security@africinemas.co.ke);
- hold seats you don't mean to buy, or use automated tools to book or to copy the
  site;
- resell tickets for more than you paid, if the cinema's terms forbid it;
- copy the site's content, except to keep a copy of your own booking.

The cinema or AfriCinemas may stop you booking, and close your account, if you do.

## 10. If something goes wrong

10.1 We work hard to keep AfriCinemas running, but it may sometimes be unavailable,
for example during maintenance or when M-Pesa is down. Our status page will be
at status.africinemas.co.ke.

10.2 The cinema is responsible for its shows, premises, snacks and refunds.
AfriCinemas is responsible for the booking system working as it should.

10.3 As far as the law allows, AfriCinemas isn't liable for loss you couldn't have
expected when you booked, or for loss caused by things beyond our reasonable control.
We don't exclude or limit any liability the law doesn't allow us to, including for
death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for fraud, or your rights under the
Consumer Protection Act.

## 11. Complaints

11.1 **About a show, a refund or the cinema**: contact the cinema first; its contacts
are on its storefront.

11.2 **About AfriCinemas** (the website, the booking system, your account): write to
help@africinemas.co.ke. We answer within 5 working days.

11.3 **About your personal data**: see our [privacy policy](/legal/privacy). You may
also complain to the **Office of the Data Protection Commissioner** (Data Protection
Act, s.56).

11.4 **If it isn't resolved**, you may go to the Small Claims Court or the other courts
of Kenya. Nothing in these terms requires you to use arbitration (Consumer Protection
Act, s.88), though you may agree to after a dispute arises.

## 12. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms. Each version has a number and the date it applies from, and
the one that applies to a booking is the one in force when you paid (it's linked in
your confirmation email). We tell account holders about important changes by email
before they apply.

## 13. Law

These terms are governed by the laws of Kenya, and the courts of Kenya can hear any
dispute about them.
