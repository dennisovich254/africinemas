# The law register

**Draft v0.1, 2026-10-26. For review by a Kenyan advocate. Not legal advice.**

Every Kenyan law the legal documents rely on, what it requires of AfriCinemas or a
cinema, and where we apply it. The **Checked** column says how each citation was
verified, so the advocate knows what to look at first:

- **Text**: read in the law's own text (the source is listed at the end).
- **Secondary**: from a law firm's or adviser's summary; the section number or detail
  needs checking against the official text.
- **To confirm**: from general knowledge; the advocate should confirm it applies and
  that the section is right.

The texts we read are the versions published by the regulator or the National Council
for Law Reporting. Each may have been amended since (the Data Protection Act's text is
the one gazetted on 11 November 2019). Checking for amendments is the first item in
[questions-for-advocate.md](questions-for-advocate.md).

| reg 6(1)(d), 6(3) | Personal data may be collected indirectly, including "information associated with web browsing"; when collected indirectly, the person is told **within 14 days**. | Browser storage ([cookie notice](cookies-and-storage.md)). | Text |
| reg 7 | **Restriction**: on the grounds listed; request on Form DPG 1; done **within 14 days**, free; refusal in writing with reasons within 14 days. | [Data requests](data-requests-procedure.md). | Text |

| reg 10 | **Rectification**: Form DPG 3; done **within 14 days**; a refusal in writing with reasons **within 7 days**; free. | [Data requests](data-requests-procedure.md). | Text |

| reg 13 | Rights exercised for others: act in the person's best interests; for a child, identify the person acting, **no profiling of a child for direct marketing**, and tell the parent the risks and safeguards. | Children; offers. | Text |
| reg 14 | Commercial purposes include direct marketing by email "about a sale". | Cinema offers. | Text |
| reg 15 | Direct marketing only where the data was collected from the person, they were told it's a purpose, they consented, there's a simple opt-out, and they haven't opted out; every message names the sender and gives a free way to stop; **an offence (up to KES 20,000 or six months) without consent**. | Offers emails: the cinema's name, the unsubscribe link (P14.3c). | Text |

| reg 22 | Automated individual decision making. | The AI assistant never decides. | Text (heading) |
| reg 23 | Publish and update a **data protection policy**: what's held, how to exercise rights, complaints, lawful purposes, transfers abroad and recipients, the retention schedule, and **collection from children** with its criteria. | The [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md) covers each item. | Text |

### The Data Protection (Registration of Data Controllers and Data Processors) Regulations, 2021

In force from 14 July 2022 (per the regulator's guidance note).

| What | Detail | Checked |
|---|---|---|
| Who must register | Private-sector controllers and processors with **annual turnover or revenue of KES 5 million or more, or more than 10 employees**, unless exempt; and, **regardless of size**, anyone processing personal data for listed activities or sectors, including **direct marketing**, hospitality, financial services, telecommunications and transport. | ODPC Guidance Note on Registration (text) |
| Fees | Between KES 4,000 and 40,000 by tier; micro and small (up to KES 5 million, 1 to 50 employees): KES 4,000, renewal KES 2,000; an entity registering as both controller and processor pays for each. | ODPC Guidance Note (text) |
| Why it matters to us | AfriCinemas sends emails about cinemas' offers on their behalf, and is a processor for every cinema, so we expect to **register as both a data controller and a data processor** whatever our size. The advocate confirms. | Our reading |

| Section | What it says | Where we apply it | Checked |
|---|---|---|---|
