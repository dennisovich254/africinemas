# Registrations checklist

**Draft v0.1, 2026-10-26. For review by a Kenyan advocate.** What AfriCinemas
registers, outside the app, before going live. In P15.0b this list becomes a checklist
in the platform console: each item ticked by a super admin, recording who ticked it,
when, and the reference (a certificate number or the advocate's note). See
[law-register.md](law-register.md) for each law.

| # | Registration | Law | Why AfriCinemas needs it | When | Reference to record |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Company** (a private company limited by shares), through the Business Registration Service on eCitizen | Companies Act, 2015 | The legal person that signs the cinema agreement, is the data controller, and invoices the fee | First | Certificate of incorporation number |
| 2 | **KRA PIN** for the company | Tax Procedures Act, 2015; Income Tax Act | Every tax obligation; on every invoice | After 1 | PIN |
| 3 | **eTIMS** (electronic tax invoices) | Tax Procedures Act, s.23; Tax Procedures (Electronic Tax Invoice) Regulations | Our invoices to cinemas must be issued through eTIMS (P15.4) | Before the first invoice | eTIMS registration |
| 4 | **VAT** | Value Added Tax Act, 2013, s.34 | Required once taxable supplies reach **KES 5 million in 12 months**; voluntary before. The advocate also checks the VAT (Digital Marketplace Supply) Regulations, 2023 | Watch the threshold monthly (P15.6's takings) | VAT number, date |
| 5 | **Data controller** registration with the ODPC | Data Protection Act, s.18; Registration Regulations, 2021 | For our own data (staff accounts, verification, billing, support). Required at KES 5 million turnover or more than 10 employees, and regardless of size if we're in a listed sector (the advocate: is recording cinemas' offers consent "direct marketing"; are cinemas "hospitality"?). Cheap, so we register before the pilot either way | Before collecting real data (the pilot) | Certificate number |
| 6 | **Data processor** registration with the ODPC | As 5 | We process every cinema's customer data. The ODPC charges a separate fee for each role | As 5 | Certificate number |
| 7 | **Data protection contact** published (or a data protection officer appointed, if required) | Data Protection Act, s.24 | The contact on the privacy policy; s.24(6) has an officer's details published and given to the Commissioner | Before the pilot | Name, email |
| 8 | **DPIA for verification**, submitted to the ODPC if s.31(5) requires it | Data Protection Act, s.31; General Regulations, regs 49 to 52 | Verification is high-risk processing; the 60-day question may set the pilot's date | **≥ 60 days before the first real verification**, if required | Submission date; ODPC reference |
| 9 | **Advice on payment-services authorisation** | National Payment System Act, 2011; NPS Regulations, 2014; *KENEX v CBK* (2022) | Whether starting M-Pesa payments for cinemas on their own paybills needs CBK authorisation | **Before 15.3 is built** | The advocate's written note |
| 10 | **Safaricom Daraja**: AfriCinemas' own paybill for collecting fees, taken live | Safaricom's terms | Cinemas pay invoices to it (P15.5) | Before the first invoice | Paybill number |
| 11 | **County single business permit** for AfriCinemas' office | [County] Finance Act | Trading in the county | After 1 | Permit number, expiry |
| 12 | **Trade mark** "AfriCinemas" (name and logo), at the Kenya Industrial Property Institute | Trade Marks Act (Cap. 506) | Protecting the name the verified badge relies on | Recommended before launch | Application number |
| 13 | **Domain** `africinemas.co.ke` held by the company, at a KeNIC-accredited registrar | KeNIC's rules | The address every storefront and the verified check page rely on | Now | Registrar, expiry |
| 14 | **The advocate's review** of the legal documents | — | Nothing is published before it | Before 15.0b publishes them | Name, firm, date, versions approved |
| 15 | **KFCB**: the advocate's view on whether AfriCinemas itself needs anything (we don't exhibit films, but storefronts show posters and trailers) | Films and Stage Plays Act, s.12, s.13 | Clarity on posters "in a public place" online | Before the pilot | The advocate's note |
| 16 | **Insurance**: professional indemnity and cyber cover | — | Covers liability under the cinema agreement and breach costs | Before the pilot | Policy numbers |
