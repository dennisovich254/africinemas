# Data requests procedure

**Draft v0.1, 2026-10-26. For review by a Kenyan advocate. Internal.** How AfriCinemas
answers people who ask about their personal data: sections 26 to 40 of the Data
Protection Act, 2019, and regulations 7 to 13 and 18 of the Data Protection (General)
Regulations, 2021. See [law-register.md](law-register.md).

## 1. Who answers

| The data | Controller | What we do |
|---|---|---|
| A cinema's customers: bookings, accounts there, payments, offers | **The cinema** | Pass the request to the cinema **within [2] working days**, tell the person we've done so, and help the cinema answer in time (cinema agreement, schedule 2.7) |
| Owners and staff accounts, verification, billing, support, our own team, platform security | **AfriCinemas** | Answer it ourselves, as below |

## 2. Receiving a request

A request may come by email to [privacy@africinemas.co.ke], through Help in the back
office, by post, or on the regulator's forms (DPG 1 to DPG 5, First Schedule of the
General Regulations). **Any form of words counts**; the person doesn't need to name the
law or use a form. A request made orally is written down and confirmed back to them.

On the day it arrives:

1. log it in the requests register: date and time received, who, what they ask, the
   deadline (section 3), the controller;
2. acknowledge it;
3. check the person's identity (section 4).

The deadline runs from the day the request arrives. Pausing it while we check identity
is a question for the advocate.

## 3. What, and by when

| Request | Law | Deadline | Fee | If we decline |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Access**: confirm whether we process their data; a copy; the purposes, categories, recipients (including abroad), retention period, and the source if not from them | s.26(b); reg 9 (Form DPG 2) | **7 days** | **Free** | In writing, with reasons |
| **Rectification** of untrue, inaccurate, outdated, incomplete or misleading data | s.26(d), s.40(1)(a); reg 10 (Form DPG 3) | **14 days** to correct | Free | **Within 7 days**, in writing, with reasons (reg 10(5)) |
| **Erasure** where the data's no longer needed, consent's withdrawn, they object and no overriding interest, direct marketing, unlawful, or a legal obligation to erase | s.26(e), s.40(1)(b); reg 12 (Form DPG 5) | **Respond within 14 days** | Free | Not where we must keep it by law (tax records), for legal claims, or the other grounds in reg 12(4); say which |
| **Restriction**: they contest accuracy; unlawful but they don't want erasure; we no longer need it but they need it for a claim; while an objection is considered | s.34; reg 7 (Form DPG 1) | **14 days** | Free | **Within 14 days**, in writing, with reasons (reg 7(6)) |
| **Objection** to processing for a purpose or in a manner | s.26(c), s.36; reg 8 (Form DPG 1) | **14 days** | Free | With reasons, and that they may complain to the Commissioner (reg 8(6)). **Direct marketing: always granted** (reg 8(4), (5)) |
| **Portability**: a structured, commonly used, machine-readable copy, or sent to another controller | s.38; reg 11 (Form DPG 4) | **30 days**, extendable for complex requests after consulting the Commissioner (s.38(7)) | A fee no higher than the cost (reg 11(4)) | **Within 7 days**, in writing, with reasons (reg 11(6)) |
| **Stop disclosure for third-party direct marketing** | reg 18 | **7 days** | Free | — (we never disclose for third-party marketing) |
| **Withdrawing consent** | s.32(2); reg 4(5) | At once | Free | — |

**Where the data was shared** with a third party (a sub-processor), and we correct or
erase it, we take reasonable steps to tell them (s.40(2)). Where erasure is asked but
the data is needed as evidence, we restrict it instead and tell the person (s.40(3)).

## 4. Checking identity

- **Signed in**: a request from inside the person's own account is theirs.
- **By email**: from the email address on the account. Otherwise, ask them to sign in,
  or to confirm a code sent to the account's email.
- **Never ask for more than needed.** Don't ask for an ID copy unless there's no other
  way.
- **On someone's behalf** (s.27; reg 13): check the authority (a parent or guardian
  for a child; a written authorisation otherwise). If unsure of the relationship, we
  may hold the request until evidence is given (reg 13(3)).

## 5. Answering

- Answer in plain words, by the same route the request came, electronically if it came
  electronically (reg 9(5)).
- Exports: a ZIP of JSON and CSV files, with a readable summary.
- Don't include other people's data. Remove it, or explain why it's withheld.
- Record what was done and when in the register.

## 6. Requests that are manifestly unfounded or excessive

A restriction request may be declined if manifestly unfounded or excessive (reg 7(5)).
For others, ask the advocate before declining. Always give reasons, and say that they
may complain to the Commissioner.

## 7. Complaints

If the person isn't satisfied, they may complain to the **Office of the Data Protection
Commissioner** (s.56). We cooperate with any investigation, and keep the register as
evidence.

## 8. In the product

Much of this can be self-service (P15.0b and later):

- staff see and change their name and password on **Your profile** (P12.15); customers
  on **My account**;
- customers withdraw their consent to a cinema's offers on **My account** (P14.3c);
- customers download their data (a JSON file: access and portability) and delete their
  account on **My account** (ADR-0088). A deletion happens seven days after it's
  asked, and can be cancelled until then. Their orders stay as the cinema's sales
  records (tax law), without the email once the showtime has passed; the M-Pesa phone
  stays with its receipt; the offers record stays, withdrawn, as proof (s.40(3)).
